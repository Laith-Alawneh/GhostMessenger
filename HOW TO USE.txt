I honestly recommend using the GUI version of the program since it is much easier to use.

Steps to use Ghost Messenger:

1. Open https://web.snapchat.com in your browser.
2. Open DevTools by pressing F12, pressing Ctrl + Shift + I, or right-clicking the page and selecting "Inspect".
3. Open the "Console" tab in the DevTools window.
4. Drag and drop ghost.gui.js into the Console, or copy everything inside the file and paste it into the Console.
5. Press Enter to run the script.

Additional Tips:

For the "Find Shortcuts" button, you first need to take a picture and click the "Send" button. This will open the screen where the shortcuts are located.

Once you are on that screen, click "Find Shortcuts". The shortcuts will then appear in the dropdown menu.

If you go back to the normal camera screen afterward, the shortcuts will still be available.
